# Staples

The fundamentals — things everyone should know how to make.

## How to Cook an Egg

### Scrambled
1. Crack 2–3 eggs into a bowl, whisk well
2. Heat pan on medium-low with a knob of butter
3. Pour eggs in and gently fold — don't stir aggressively
4. Remove from heat just before they look fully done (residual heat finishes them)
5. Season with salt and pepper

### Fried
1. Heat oil or butter in a pan on medium
2. Crack egg directly into pan
3. Cook until whites are set but yolk is still runny (for sunny-side up)
4. Season to taste

### Boiled
- Soft boil: 6–7 minutes in boiling water
- Hard boil: 10–12 minutes

## How to Make Bread

### Basic Loaf
**Ingredients:** 500g flour, 7g yeast, 1 tsp salt, 300ml warm water, 1 tbsp olive oil

1. Combine flour, yeast, and salt in a bowl
2. Add warm water and oil, mix to form a dough
3. Knead for 10 minutes until smooth and elastic
4. Cover and let rise for 1 hour (until doubled)
5. Knock back, shape, and place in a loaf tin
6. Second rise: 30–45 minutes
7. Bake at 220°C for 25–30 minutes
8. Tap the bottom — should sound hollow when done
