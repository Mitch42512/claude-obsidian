# 04. XP Goal Setting App (Idea)

## Overview
A gamified goal-setting and habit tracker that rewards users with XP and levels them up as they hit milestones. Makes personal growth feel like an RPG.

## Goals
- Set personal goals and break them into tasks
- Earn XP for completing tasks
- Level up and unlock rewards or badges
