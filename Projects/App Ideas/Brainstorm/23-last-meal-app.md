# Last Meal App 🍽️

## Concept

An app built around the idea of "lasts" — last meals, last movies, last countries visited, last something. A mix of curiosity, nostalgia, and connection.

## Core Ideas

- Browse videos / content of celebrities' last meals
- See what famous people chose as their last meal, last movie, last destination
- Users can log their own "lasts" and share them
- Discover people with similar tastes through their last experiences

## Possible Categories

- Last meal
- Last movie watched
- Last country visited
- Last book read
- Last song played

## Social Hook

- Connect with people over shared "last" experiences
- "You both last watched Parasite — you might get along"
