# 18. Quotes & Vocabulary App

## Overview
A phone homescreen widget app that updates with a new quote or vocabulary word each time. Users choose their learning category — vocabulary, motivational quotes, language learning, etc.

## Goals
- Display a new quote or word on the phone homescreen widget
- Let users pick what type of content they want to see
- Refresh with each open or on a schedule

## Features
- Category selector — vocabulary, quotes, language, custom topics
- Homescreen widget that updates dynamically
- Daily or per-open refresh logic
