# 06. Personal Health App (Idea)

## Overview
A personal health tracking app for logging symptoms, appointments, and general wellbeing. Acts as a private health logbook.

## Goals
- Log symptoms grouped by issue or body area
- Track appointments (GP, physio, dentist, skin check)
- Set reminders for recurring health checks
