# Minimalistic Poker

## Concept

A stripped-back, beautifully minimal take on poker. Remove the noise — keep only the essential decisions and tension of the game.

## Core Idea

Replicate this "minimalistic" concept across different game types. What other games could be distilled to their purest, most essential form?

## Variations to Explore

- Minimalistic Chess
- Minimalistic Blackjack
- Minimalistic Mahjong
- Minimalistic Rummy

## Design Principles

- No clutter — only what's needed to play
- Clean typography and layout
- Fast to pick up, deep strategy underneath
