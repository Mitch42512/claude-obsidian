# 17. Frog Empire Mailing List

## Overview
A monthly newsletter built around the Frog Empire — an illustrated universe where each issue introduces a new frog character. Every frog has a name, a home, a backstory, and original artwork.

## Goals
- Build and grow a subscriber mailing list
- Release one new frog character per month
- Develop a consistent world and lore that evolves over time

## The World
- The Frog Empire is a living, expanding universe
- Each frog has a distinct home region, personality, and role in the empire
- Stories and lore build on each other month to month

## Monthly Issue Structure
- Character name and title
- Where they live (region/environment within the Frog Empire)
- Background — who they are, what they do, their personality
- Original artwork of the frog
- A small story snippet or world update

## Tech & Tools
- Mailing platform — e.g. Resend, Mailchimp, or Buttondown
- Landing page for signups
- CMS or simple doc system for drafting each issue
- Archive page so new subscribers can read back issues
