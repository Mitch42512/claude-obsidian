# 19. Cold Call Website Builder

## Overview
A tool that generates niche, localised business directory websites (e.g. "Plumbers in Eastwood"). The sites attract inbound calls from people in that area, which are then sold to relevant service providers or hubs.

## Goals
- Programmatically generate local service directory pages
- Drive organic traffic through SEO-optimised local pages
- Monetise by selling inbound calls to local businesses

## How It Works
1. Pick a service category and suburb (e.g. electricians in Parramatta)
2. Generate a landing page with relevant local info and a call number
3. Calls routed through a tracked number
4. Sell those call leads to local tradespeople or service hubs

## Ideas
- Start with one niche and one suburb to validate
- Use call tracking tools (e.g. CallRail or similar)
- Expand to multiple industries and locations once proven
- Could be white-labelled or sold as a service to local businesses
