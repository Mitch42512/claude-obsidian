# Granny's Tips App 👵

## Concept

An app that collects traditional home remedies and life hacks passed down through generations — the kind of practical wisdom that grandmothers around the world swear by.

## Examples

- 🍋 Italy: Use lemon when you're sick
- 🧴 Washing sheets with vinegar to remove smells
- More cultural tips to collect and verify

## Features to Consider

- Browse tips by country / culture
- Browse by category (health, cleaning, cooking, etc.)
- Submit your own granny tip
- Upvote / verify tips
- Source attribution (region, culture)
