# Best of Apps 🏆

## Concept

A recommendation app with a Hinge / Tinder-style UI — but instead of dating, people discover the best of anything. Users pick a topic, curate their personal top 5, and share it with the world.

## How It Works

1. Pick a topic (e.g. "Hot Cross Buns in Sydney", "Croissants in Melbourne")
2. Browse and rate places / items in that category
3. Each place has a clickable icon showing its community rank
4. View a map of the best spots
5. See specific people's top 5 or a worldwide top 5

## Example Use Cases

- Best hot cross buns in Sydney
- Best croissants
- Best ramen spots in Tokyo
- Best bookshops in London

## Features to Consider

- Map view with pins for each entry
- User profiles with their top 5 lists per category
- Swipe mechanic to rate options (better vs worse)
- Worldwide aggregated rankings
- Category browser / search
- Social — follow people with similar taste
