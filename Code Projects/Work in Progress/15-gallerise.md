# 15. Gallerise

## Overview
A 3D walkable pixel art gallery platform. Users get their own gallery space, a pixel avatar, and can browse, follow, and trade art with other creators.

## Goals
- Let artists curate and display pixel art in a 3D walkable room
- Build a social feed (art guide) for following creators
- Create an in-app credit economy with an auction house for buying/selling art

## Features
- Pixel avatar per user — your identity on the platform
- Gallery in two modes: Curator (edit) and Public (view)
- Live visitor counter + NPC ghost system to make rooms feel alive
- Upload art with canvas size, title, description, and optional sale toggle
- Art Guide — Instagram-style feed of new uploads from followed galleries
- Auction House — bid on exclusive art using earned credits
- Credits earned through gallery visits and direct donations on art pieces
- Admin page — toggle public/private, add gallery rooms, swap avatar, unlock milestones
- Progression system — unlock exclusive items by hitting upload and visitor milestones

## Tech
- Google OAuth for login
- 3D gallery rendering (web-based)
- Internal credit/economy system
