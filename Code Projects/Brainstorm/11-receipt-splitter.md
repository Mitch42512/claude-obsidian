# 11. Receipt Splitter

## Overview
An app for splitting bills and receipts among a group of people. Calculates who owes what and simplifies shared expenses.

## Goals
- Upload or manually enter receipt items
- Assign items to individuals in a group
- Calculate each person's share and total
